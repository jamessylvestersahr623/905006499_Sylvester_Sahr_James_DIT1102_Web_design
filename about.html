Seasonal Harvest

PORTLAND, OREGON LOCAL GROWERS

Field & Fork brings the neighbourhood farmers market online. We connect everyday shoppers with nearby growers, making fresh vegetables and pantry grains easy to find in one place.

From Green Valley Farm tomatoes to Golden Fields whole wheat, every listing puts the farm front and centre. You know where your food comes from, and growers retain more local profit.
